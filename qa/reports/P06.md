Completion uses one atomic transaction rather than a partially committed workflow: a failure before commit leaves the session active, with no final outcomes, absences or completed-class increment. Retrying recomputes the operation. Once the completion marker commits, repeated close returns without recalculating outcomes or overwriting corrections. This uses Firebase's documented [atomic transactions and retry semantics](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/manage-data/transactions) and [transaction isolation](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/transaction-data-contention).

Root/nested summaries explicitly store `final_status`, `credited`, `finalization_version: 1`, and compatible status/flags. Present and Excused contribute one class; left_early and absent contribute zero. `students_present`/`student_count` mean credited membership including Excused. Each eligible student has a root/nested outcome, including zero-observation B. Noncredited outcomes have a deterministic absence projection; credited corrections remove stale absences. Student attendance/absence contributions change once. Missing legacy absence counters are initialized from distinct completed-session historical absence records (module/student aliases supported), then adjusted for this class. Current enrollment is not used to rewrite class eligibility.

Corrections require completed eligible evidence and the actual owner. They preserve observations and append actor/before/after/reason/time audit when status changes. Repeating a coherent version 1 status is a no-op, including saved timestamps; concurrent identical corrections produce one audit transition. The existing Late compatibility path is retained without establishing a new Late business policy. Deleted profiles are not recreated; their saved UID/registration outcomes and corrections remain historical evidence.
