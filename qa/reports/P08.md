## Limitations and verification

- Tests use WidgetTester gestures/text on Android and React/jsdom. Native Android IME competed with test text in an initial attempt; the test binding now registers Flutter's test keyboard while retaining real HTTP. This verifies form logic, not native keyboard behavior. See [Flutter TestTextInput documentation](https://api.flutter.dev/flutter/flutter_test/TestTextInput/enterText.html). An initial busy-modal `pumpAndSettle` hang and the stream-context defect were corrected before final passes; failed attempts are not acceptance evidence.
- Full browser/mobile navigation journeys, accessibility, goldens, physical BLE, real-user usability, actual-device reconnect/radio capacity, load, CI and final full regression remain unrun. P09/P12 and later phases require separate authorization. Student Windows desktop testing stays explicitly deferred; iOS was not tested.
- Full frontend lint still reports AuthContext/StudentsContext/ModulesContext fast-refresh exports, ModulesContext memo/dependency findings, unused ModuleDetails values/effect dependencies, and conditional hooks/impure rendering in unmounted legacy AddSession/EditSession/EditModule/DashboardHome. These are recorded limits, not a clean-lint claim. Broad mobile analysis was not completed as a clean gate; focused analysis and both Android builds are the evidence here.
- Fault injection models individual SDK operation failure, not every crash point or a network outage. Persistent retry and historical preservation are checked against local storage; production, continuous physical presence and real hardware behavior are unverified.
